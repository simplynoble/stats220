Tracking my YouTube watching habits

Over a two-week period in March 2026, I logged every YouTube video I watched using a Google Form. Each entry captured the video category, length in minutes, how I found the video, and a personal rating out of 10. The goal was to uncover patterns in what I watch, how I find content, and whether I actually enjoy what the algorithm serves me.

At a glance

Over the logging period, I watched a total of 21 videos. The average video length was 38.1 minutes, and my average rating was 6.4 out of 10. Notably, 71.4% of videos I watched came from YouTube’s homepage or recommendation algorithm.

What am I watching?

Music videos made up the largest share of my viewing, followed by Entertainment/Comedy and News/Politics. Education and Self Help content appeared less frequently, while Technology had only a single entry.

How am I finding these videos?

The YouTube homepage and recommendation algorithm dominated how I found content, accounting for 71.4% of all videos. Searching and shared links were more common for News/Politics and Self Help content, suggesting I actively seek out those categories rather than passively consuming them.
